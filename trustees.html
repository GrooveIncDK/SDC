YFPCloud Cutover Plan — summary for Trustees and the formation committee

This page summarises, for Trustees and the SDC formation committee, the plan to move SDC's shared workspace (YFPCloud) onto its permanent home and bring Trustee access under proper governance. It leaves out internal engineering detail — infrastructure configuration, credentials handling and incident logs — which the development team keeps in its own internal records. For the full working document, ask a member of the development team for access.

Related: the SDC Trustee Appointment Minutes Template, used to minute and reference every Trustee appointment or removal.

Target outcome

When this plan is complete, every YFPCloud user signs in through SDC's single shared identity system, at its permanent address, and the old legacy environment is retired. YFPCloud becomes a shared governance space for Groove Inc, SDC, YFP UK, YFP Uganda and other organisations — Trustees use it to oversee SDC's delivery of services to farmers registered on YFP.

  • The old legacy environment is decommissioned.
  • The current staging environment is promoted to permanent, production use.
  • All logins go through the shared identity system. One recovery-only administrator account is kept for emergencies.
  • The identity system holds a Trustees group and organisation groups (Groove Inc, SDC, YFP UK, YFP Uganda), used to control who can see what.
  • Trustee registration is a separate, invite-only path, distinct from ordinary user signup.

Phases

Six phases run in order; each needs explicit sign-off before it begins.

  1. Phase 0 — Inventory the legacy environment. List users, files, boards and shares. Decide what migrates and what is archived.
  2. Phase 1 — Groups in the identity system. Add Trustees and the organisation groups (Groove Inc, SDC, YFP UK, YFP Uganda) so access can be controlled by group membership.
  3. Phase 2 — Trustee invite and signup path. Build invite issuance, the invite-only Trustee signup page and the appointment audit trail described below.
  4. Phase 3 — Domain cutover. Point the platform's permanent address to the new environment and update internal configuration accordingly.
  5. Phase 4 — Login and group mapping. Enforce sign-in through the shared identity system, with one recovery-only administrator account. Restrict the development backlog to the Groove Inc group.
  6. Phase 5 — Migrate, parallel-run, decommission. Migrate data and the backlog, run both environments side by side for an agreed window, then decommission the old environment.

Trustee onboarding (actually sending invites) waits for the SDC–YFP data processing agreement, whatever phase the build has reached.

Trustee invite audit trail

Every route into the Trustees group leaves an immutable, attributable record. This is the governance design; the engineering build is tracked separately.

  • Committee approval is mandatory. An invite cannot be issued without a reference to the SDC formation committee's approval (decision date and minute reference) — see the Appointment Minutes Template.
  • The data processing agreement blocks issuance. Invites are refused until an SDC–YFP agreement reference is recorded, enforcing Q3 above in the system itself, not just by procedure.
  • Records are append-only. Each invite gets one record. Every change of state (issued, redeemed, expired, revoked) adds a new entry with who made it and when. Nothing is edited or deleted.
  • Invite links are single-use and time-limited, and the system never stores the raw link — only enough to verify it once, when used.
  • Redemption is the only route into Trustees. Using an invite records the resulting account and grants the group. Revoking an invite or a membership removes the group and writes its own record.
  • Every change is logged a second time, independently of the record above, so the trail exists in two places.

Decided 1 Nov 2026. Invites are issued only by a system administrator, through a restricted internal tool. The link is shown once on screen and sent by the issuer. A Trustee account's email must match the invited address exactly (ignoring case), for new and existing accounts alike.

Status

  • Phase 0 — complete and signed off, 31 Oct 2026. Legacy account cleanup is done: redundant accounts removed, shared boards transferred to the new administrator account, and departing users' files retained for return once they have accounts on the new platform.
  • Phase 1 — complete, 31 Oct 2026. Trustee and organisation groups are built and verified in the development environment.
  • Phases 2–5 have not yet started.

Open questions

Unanswered, and need a decision before the phase that depends on them.

  • How long is the parallel-run window in Phase 5?
  • Are more organisation groups needed now (other YFP regional subsidiaries), or only the four named?
