Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Sustainable Development Crescent (SDC) respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain terms, what personal information we collect through this website, why, and the rights you have over it.

1. Who We Are

Sustainable Development Crescent (SDC) is a charitable incorporated organisation. For this website, SDC is the data controller responsible for your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

This website is mainly informational. We keep what we collect to a minimum:

  • If you contact us, we collect your name, email address and the content of your message.
  • Basic technical information (such as browser type and general location) is generated automatically by visiting the site, as with most websites.

We don't ask visitors to create an account, and we don't knowingly collect sensitive personal information through this site.

3. How We Use It

We use the information we collect only to:

  • respond to your enquiry or message
  • keep the records we reasonably need for our own administration
  • occasionally share updates about our work, if you've asked us to

We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it for anything unrelated to these purposes.

4. Cookies

This site uses only the cookies technically necessary for it to work correctly. We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

5. Sharing Your Information

We don't routinely share your information with third parties. We may share it where necessary to comply with the law, to protect SDC's rights, or with service providers who help us run this website (for example, hosting).

6. How Long We Keep It

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected for, and delete or anonymise it once that purpose has passed.

7. Your Rights

Subject to the usual legal conditions, you may have the right to ask us to:

  • show you the personal information we hold about you
  • correct it if it's inaccurate
  • delete it
  • stop or restrict how we use it

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, if you're unhappy with how we've handled your information.

8. Children's Privacy

This website isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this page from time to time. The current version is always the one published here.
